---
title: Posting a job
description: "Step by step: how to add a job as admin."
---

Jobs you post as admin are **free** and go live **immediately**. They do not need approval.

## Open the form

1. Log in.
2. Click **Admin** in the top bar.
3. Click **Post a new job** (top right of the Manage jobs page).

## Fill in the form

The form has five sections.

**Basic info**
- **Job title**: required.
- **Company**: required.
- **Company logo**: optional picture. PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG.
- **Job category**: pick one from the list.
- **Description**: required. Write what the job involves. Line breaks are kept.

**Location & type**
- **Location type**: Remote, Hybrid or On-site.
- **Employment type**: Full-time, Part-time, Contract or Internship.
- **Location**: pick a place from the list, or choose **Other** and type your own. Required.
- **Tags (comma separated)**: for example `sales, hindi`. People can search by these.

**Compensation**
- **Salary min (USD)** and **Salary max (USD)**: optional. Shown on the job card.

**How to apply**
- **External apply URL (optional)** and **Apply email (optional)**: if you fill these, the job page also shows "Prefer to apply directly?" with those links. The normal apply form still works.
- **Screening questions (optional, one per line)**: each line becomes a question that applicants must answer.

**Company verification**
- **Company address**, **Company email** and **Company phone number**: for your records.

## Save

Click **Post job** at the bottom. You go back to **Manage jobs** and the job appears in the list with the status **PUBLISHED**. It is now visible on the public **Browse jobs** page.

<Callout type="warn">
Changing the screening questions after people have applied deletes their answers to the old questions. Add questions before the job goes live.
</Callout>
