365DaysJobsTeam is a job board at **365daysjobs.com**. Employers post jobs, job seekers apply to them, and you (the admin) keep everything running.

This guide only covers what you see on screen: which button to press and where to go. It is visible to admins only.

## The three kinds of accounts

| Account | Who they are | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Job seeker** | People looking for work | Browse jobs for free. Buy a plan to apply. Track their applications. |
| **Employer** | Companies hiring | Buy the employer plan. Post jobs, edit or close them, see applicants and update their status. |
| **Admin** | You | Post jobs for free, approve employer jobs, manage every job, and see all applicants. |

People choose **Job seeker** or **Employer** when they sign up. Nobody can sign up as an admin.

## What each person sees in the top bar

| Account | Top bar buttons |
|---|---|
| Not logged in | Browse jobs, Log in, Sign up |
| Job seeker | Browse jobs, Pricing, My applications, Sign out |
| Employer | Browse jobs, My jobs, Sign out |
| Admin | Browse jobs, Admin, My applications, Sign out |

At the bottom of every page there are links to **Terms & Conditions**, **Privacy Policy**, **Refund Policy** and **Contact**.
