title: Logging in
description: How to log in as admin, reset your password, and get around.
---

## Log in as admin

1. Go to **365daysjobs.com** and click **Log in** in the top bar.
2. Type your admin email and password.
3. Click **Log in**.

You land on the **Manage jobs** page. The top bar now shows **Browse jobs**, **Admin**, **My applications** and **Sign out**.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the Log in page, click **Forgot password?** under the password box.
2. Type your email and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open your email inbox. Look for **"Reset your password"** from 365DaysJobsTeam.
4. Click **Reset password** in that email.
5. Type your new password (at least 8 characters) and save.
6. You are logged in and taken to your dashboard.

<Callout type="warn">
Open the email link in the **same browser** you used to ask for the reset. If it says the link is invalid or expired, ask for a new one. Each link works only once.
</Callout>

## Move around the admin area

Once you click **Admin** in the top bar, a small menu above the page has three items:

- **Jobs** goes to the Manage jobs page.
- **Settings** switches the employer plan on or off. See [Settings](/docs/settings).
- **Docs** opens this guide.

## Sign out

Click **Sign out** in the top bar, the black button on the right.
