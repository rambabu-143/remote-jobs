## Open it

1. Log in as admin.
2. Click **Admin** in the top bar.
3. Click **Settings** in the small menu above the page (next to **Jobs** and **Docs**).

## The employer plan switch

The page shows one setting: **Require the employer plan (₹499/month)**. A badge on the right tells you the current state: **Free posting** or **Plan required**. The button at the bottom switches it.

| | **Free posting** (the starting setting) | **Plan required** |
|---|---|---|
| Employers see the ₹499 plan | No, it is hidden | Yes, on **My jobs** |
| Employers can post jobs | Yes, with no payment | Only after they subscribe |
| A new job goes to | **Pending**, waiting for you | **Pending** if they have a plan, **Draft** if not |
| Live jobs of an employer whose plan ended | Stay visible | Hidden until they renew |
| Plan reminder emails to employers | Not sent | Sent |

**In both cases every job still waits for your approval.** You are the only one who can make a job live.

## Switching

- Click **Require the employer plan** to start charging. Employers who already have a plan keep it, and their dates do not change.
- Click **Switch to free posting** to stop charging. Any jobs employers saved as drafts (waiting for a plan) are sent to you for review straight away, so none get stuck.

The change takes effect at once for everyone. Nothing is deleted when you switch back and forth: jobs, accounts and plan dates are kept.

Job seeker plans (₹49, ₹249, ₹399) are not affected by this setting.
