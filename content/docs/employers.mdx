---
title: Guide for employers
description: How an employer signs up, subscribes, posts and manages jobs. Use it to help employers.
---

## Sign up

1. Click **Sign up** in the top bar.
2. Choose **Employer** (not Job seeker).
3. Fill in your name, email and password, then click **Sign up**.
4. Open the confirmation email, click **Confirm email address**, and you arrive on **My jobs**, already logged in.

The top bar for an employer shows **Browse jobs**, **Post a job**, **My jobs**, **Contact** and **Sign out**.

## Subscribe (only when the plan is required)

You decide in [Settings](/docs/settings) whether employers pay. When it is **free posting** (the starting setting), employers see no plan and nothing to buy. When the **plan is required**, employers pay for a plan, not for each job: **₹499** gives **30 days** of unlimited job posts.

1. Click **My jobs** in the top bar.
2. If a plan is needed, you see an **Employer plan** box. Click **Subscribe**.
3. Pay in the payment window. When it finishes, the page says **"Your employer plan is active until [date]"**.

The plan does not renew by itself. After 30 days the employer must subscribe again.

## Post a job

1. Click **Post a job** in the top bar (or the **Post a job** button on **My jobs**).
2. Fill in the form (the same fields as in [Posting a job](/docs/posting-jobs)) and click **Post job**.
3. What happens next:
   - **Free posting**, or **plan active**: the job goes straight to **Pending review**.
   - **Plan required and no plan yet**: the job is saved as **"Draft, subscribe to submit"**. As soon as they subscribe, all their drafts move to **Pending review** automatically.
4. After the admin approves it, the job shows as **Live** and appears on the public site.

## Manage jobs

Each job on **My jobs** has buttons:

- **Edit**: change the job. A live job goes back to **Pending review** after saving.
- **Close**: takes a live job down. **Reopen** puts it back.
- **Applicants**: see applicants and change their status.

Screening questions cannot be changed once someone has applied.

## When the plan runs out (only when the plan is required)

The employer's live jobs show **"Hidden, plan expired"** and disappear from the public. Nothing is deleted. When they subscribe again, the jobs come back at once. They get an email 3 days before the plan ends and another after it ends.
