---
title: Emails the site sends
description: Every email, who gets it, and when.
---

All emails come from **365DaysJobsTeam** at the address `noreply@mail.365daysjobs.com`. They have a black header, a button, and links to the Terms and Privacy pages at the bottom.

## Account emails

| Email | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirm your email address** | The new person | Right after they sign up. The button logs them in. |
| **Reset your password** | The person asking | When they use **Forgot password?** |

## Hiring emails

| Email | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| **New application: [job]** | The employer (or admin) who posted the job | Someone applies. It has a **Review applicants** button. |
| **Your application for [job] was updated** | The applicant | An applicant's status is changed. |
| **New job waiting for approval** | All admins | An employer's job is ready for review, or a live job was edited. |
| **Your job is live** | The employer | You click **Approve**. |
| **Listing not approved** | The employer | You click **Reject**. |

## Plan emails

| Email | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Your plan ends soon** | Seekers and employers | A plan has up to 3 days left |
| **Your plan has ended** | Seekers and employers | The day after a plan ends |

## If someone says they did not get an email

1. Ask them to check **Spam** or **Promotions**.
2. Check the address they used is spelled correctly.
3. Ask them to try again after a few minutes.
