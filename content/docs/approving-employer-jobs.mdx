---
title: Approving employer jobs
description: What to do when an employer's job is waiting for you.
---

Every job an employer posts must be approved by you before the public can see it.

## How you find out

When an employer's job is ready for review, an email arrives at the admin address:

- **"New job waiting for approval: [title]"** for one job, or
- **"2 new jobs waiting for approval"** (and so on) if several were submitted together.

The email has a **Review now** button that opens the Manage jobs page.

## Approve or reject

1. Click **Admin** in the top bar.
2. Look for rows whose **Status** says **PENDING**.
3. Click **Edit** if you want to read the full job first (go back without saving if you only wanted to look).
4. Click **Approve** to put it live, or **Reject** to turn it down.

## What happens next

- **Approve**: the job becomes **PUBLISHED** and shows on Browse jobs straight away. The employer gets an email: "Your job is live".
- **Reject**: the job becomes **REJECTED** and stays hidden. The employer gets an email saying it wasn't approved and that they can post a corrected listing while their plan is active.

Clicking Approve or Reject twice, or from two screens, only counts once.

## What employers can do after approval

If an employer **edits** a live job, it goes back to **PENDING** and disappears from the public until you approve it again. You get a new "waiting for approval" email. This stops anyone getting a harmless job approved and then changing it.
