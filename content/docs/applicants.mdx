---
title: Applicants
description: See who applied, read their answers, open their resume and update their status.
---

## Open the list

**As admin:**
1. Click **Admin** in the top bar.
2. On the **Manage jobs** page, click the number in the **Applicants** column of the job.

**As an employer:**
1. Click **My jobs** in the top bar.
2. On a live or closed job, click **Applicants**.

The page is titled **Applicants for [job title]**.

## What you see for each applicant

- Their name and email.
- **View resume**: opens their resume in a new tab (PDF or Word). Only the applicant, the employer who posted the job, and admins can open it.
- Their cover note, if they wrote one.
- Their answers to your screening questions, each under its question.
- A **status box** on the right.

## Change an applicant's status

Click the status box and pick one:

| Status | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **PENDING** | Not looked at yet (the starting status) |
| **REVIEWED** | You have seen their application |
| **ACCEPTED** | You want them |
| **REJECTED** | Not a fit |

It saves the moment you pick it. There is no Save button. The applicant is emailed about the change and sees the new status on their **My applications** page.

Employers can only change applicants on **their own** jobs. Admins can change any applicant.
