---
title: Guide for job seekers
description: How a job seeker signs up, finds jobs, buys a plan and applies. Use it to help seekers.
---

## Sign up

1. Click **Sign up** in the top bar.
2. Choose **Job seeker**.
3. Fill in your name, email and password, then click **Sign up**.
4. Open the confirmation email and click **Confirm email address**. You arrive on **My applications**, logged in.

The top bar shows **Browse jobs**, **Pricing**, **My applications**, **Contact** and **Sign out**.

## Find jobs (free)

1. Click **Browse jobs**.
2. Use the search box ("Search title, company, or tag") and the four filters: **Any employment type**, **Any category**, **Any location**, and **Any time** (pick Past 24 hours, Past 3 days, Past week, Past 2 weeks or Past month to see only recent jobs).
3. Click **Search**.
4. Click a job to read it.

Browsing never needs a plan.

## Buy a plan to apply

You need an active plan to see the apply form.

1. Click **Pricing**.
2. Choose **1 Month (₹49)**, **6 Months (₹249)** or **1 Year (₹399)** and click **Subscribe**.
3. Pay in the window that opens. Pricing then says **"Your access is active until [date]"**.

## Apply

1. Open a job. The black **Apply** button is at the top right, opposite the job title (on a phone it sits under the title).
2. Click **Apply**:
   - If you are not logged in, you go to the log in page.
   - If you have no plan, you go to **Pricing** to pick one.
   - If you have a plan, the page jumps down to the **Apply for this job** form below the description.
3. In the form, upload your **resume** (PDF or Word, up to 4 MB). This is required.
4. Add a **cover note** if you like.
5. Answer every screening question. They are all required.
6. Click **Submit application**.

If the company also lists a website or email, you will see **Prefer to apply directly?** under the form.

After you apply, the Apply button is replaced by **Applied · PENDING**. You cannot apply to the same job twice.

## Track your applications

Click **My applications**. Every job you applied to is listed with its status: **Pending**, **Reviewed**, **Accepted** or **Rejected**. You also get an email each time the status changes.

When your plan has ended, this page shows **"No active plan. See plans"**.
