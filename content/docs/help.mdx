title: Common questions
description: Quick answers when something looks wrong.

## A job I posted is not on the Browse jobs page

- Look at its **Status** on Manage jobs. Only **PUBLISHED** jobs show to the public.
- If it is an employer's job, the employer's plan may have ended. The job comes back when they renew.
- If you just posted it, refresh the page once.

## An employer says their job is not showing

Ask them to open **My jobs** and read the label on the job:

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft, subscribe to submit** | They have no active plan. They need to subscribe. |
| **Pending review** | Waiting for you to approve it. |
| **Live** | It is showing. |
| **Hidden, plan expired** | Their plan ended. They need to subscribe again. |
| **Rejected** | You turned it down. |
| **Closed** | They (or you) took it down. They can click **Reopen**. |

## A job seeker cannot see the apply form

They need an active plan. They should click **Pricing** and subscribe. (Employer accounts cannot apply to jobs at all, so they never see the apply form.)

## Someone paid but their plan did not start

1. Ask them to refresh the page and check **Pricing** or **My jobs** once.
2. Find the payment in the Razorpay dashboard under **Transactions**. If it says **Captured**, the plan should start by itself within a minute.
3. If it still does not start, or the payment shows as failed or missing, ask them to try again, or refund them from Razorpay.

## Someone signed up as the wrong kind of account

An account cannot be switched on screen. Ask them to sign up again with a different email and choose the right type.

## I forgot the admin password

On the Log in page, click **Forgot password?** and use the admin email. See [Logging in](/docs/logging-in).

## An applicant's resume will not open

The **View resume** link only works while you are logged in as the admin, or as the employer who owns the job. If you are logged in and it still fails, ask the applicant to apply again with a PDF or Word file under 4 MB.
