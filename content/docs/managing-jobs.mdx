## Open it

Click **Admin** in the top bar. You land on **Manage jobs**.

## The table

Every job on the site is listed, from admins and employers, with these columns:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Title** and **Company** | What the job is |
| **Status** | Where the job is in its life (see below) |
| **Applicants** | How many people applied. Click the number to see them. |

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Visible to the public? |
|---|---|---|
| **PUBLISHED** | Live | Yes |
| **PENDING** | An employer paid and submitted it. Waiting for you. | No |
| **DRAFT** | An employer saved it but has no active plan yet (only happens while the employer plan is required) | No |
| **REJECTED** | You turned it down | No |
| **CLOSED** | Taken down (by you or the employer) | No |

While the employer plan is required, an employer's PUBLISHED job is also hidden from the public when their plan has run out. It comes back when they renew.

## The buttons on each row

- **Edit**: opens the same form as posting, filled in. Click **Save changes** when done. Editing a job keeps its status.
- **Approve** and **Reject**: only on PENDING jobs. See [Approving employer jobs](/docs/approving-employer-jobs).
- **Deactivate**: takes a live job down (status becomes CLOSED). The job and its applicants are kept.
- **Activate**: puts a CLOSED job back live.
- **Delete**: removes the job **for good**, with its applicants and their answers. There is no "are you sure?" message, so be careful.
