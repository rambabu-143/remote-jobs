## Prices

| Who | Plan | Price | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job seeker | 1 Month | ₹49 | 1 month |
| Job seeker | 6 Months | ₹249 | 6 months |
| Job seeker | 1 Year | ₹399 | 12 months |
| Employer | Employer plan | ₹499 | 30 days |

- Admins never pay.
- Nothing renews automatically. When a plan ends, the person subscribes again if they want to.
- Buying again **before** the plan ends adds the new time on top of what is left.
- All payments are processed by Razorpay.

## What a plan ending does

- **Job seekers** can still browse but cannot apply until they renew.
- **Employers** have their live jobs hidden from the public until they renew. Jobs are not deleted.

## Reminder emails

Every morning the site checks for plans that are ending:

- **"Your plan ends soon"**, when a plan has up to 3 days left.
- **"Your plan has ended"**, the day after it ends.

Each person gets each reminder once. If they renew, the reminders start over for the new end date.

## Refunds

The site has a **Refund Policy** page (link at the bottom of every page). In short: refunds are for duplicate payments or payments that did not activate the plan. Activated plans are not refunded.

**To refund someone**, you do it in the Razorpay dashboard:
1. Log in to Razorpay.
2. Open **Transactions** and find the payment.
3. Open it and click **Refund**.

The site does not change the person's plan when you refund. If you refund a payment, tell the person their access is ending.
